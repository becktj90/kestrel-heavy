## Branches

- `scrub` (terminal) — Scrub ends the count. Propellant cost is refunded in Kc; development cost is not.
- `abort.pad` (ignition) — Pad abort when thrust or the release commit fails and the vehicle is still on the pad.
- `recycle` (terminal) — Recycle sends the count back to the terminal count after a thrust failure before release.
- `abort.maxq` (ascent) — Abort when dynamic pressure exceeds the vehicle limit at Max-Q.
