## Optional paid upgrades (ask Trevor, not approved)

Nothing below is approved or purchased. One ask, two options; either replaces or polishes the $0 MIDI score, not required to ship.

1. **Commissioned composer (work-for-hire, full buyout).** Indie freelance game composers most commonly quote about **$400 per finished minute** (2025 GameSoundCon survey: indie median $407/min, average $618/min, top end $2,100/min). This score needs roughly 12–18 finished minutes of music plus stems → **about $5k–$7k at the common rate, $7k–$11k at the average**. Contract must be work-for-hire or full assignment, include stems and adaptive cut-downs, warrant originality (no sound-alikes), and allow use in a paid iOS app. Source: https://www.gamesoundcon.com/post/gamesoundcon-game-audio-industry-survey-2025
2. **Paid AI music plan (Suno Pro).** $10/month (or $8/month billed yearly); commercial rights for songs generated **and downloaded** while subscribed persist after cancelling; 20 commercial downloads/month. Premier is $30/month ($24 yearly). One month of Pro (~$10) would also let Trevor regenerate a commercially licensed title theme. Caveats: free-plan songs (possibly the current `theme`) stay non-commercial; output must still be checked for unintended resemblance; stems only on Premier. Sources: https://suno.com/pricing, https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2410177, https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2425729

Prices checked 2026-10-11 and may change.
