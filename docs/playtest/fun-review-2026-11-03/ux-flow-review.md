# UX flow fun review: Kestrel Heavy, 2026-11-03

Kestrel UX's part of the Nov 3 fun review. This covers flow, friction and dead time from first launch through KH-1, KH-2 and KH-3, plus the designer. QA's bot play-throughs are separate. This file doesn't repeat them.

## How this was seen

- **Build:** the WebGL artifact `webgl-build` from the green **main** run 41556949101, at main `30262fe9` ("Boost liftoff/max-Q screen shake…", #412). It was downloaded free with `gh run download` and served on localhost with its Brotli headers.
- **Driver:** headless Chrome (puppeteer-core) with SwiftShader WebGL and real mouse clicks on the canvas. Each run used a fresh profile, so each started from a fresh save.
  - **Portrait, 390x844:** Home, KH-1 (flown twice), KH-2, KH-3, a deliberate failed KH-3 (1 stage), and Fix it.
  - **Landscape web, 1440x900:** Home, Settings, Show full designer, KH-1 in the full designer, and Fix it on a pass.
- **Code:** read on main `30262fe9` for line references. Each finding is labelled **seen in build** or **from code only**.
- **Not seen, and not claimed:**
  - No iPhone or iPad device and no iOS build.
  - No audio judgement, because the run was headless.
  - No VoiceOver.
  - No real network download time. The build is 34 MB, and loading it from localhost took 4.5 s.
  - Thrum is off by default, so the guide wasn't reviewed.
  - Missions after KH-3 weren't played.
  - Timings are approximate. SwiftShader rendered at roughly 0.7x game speed, so durations below are game seconds read from the T+ clock or the code.
- **Screenshots:** in `ux-shots/`, downscaled with a 160-colour palette.

## First-minute hook verdict: it doesn't land yet

**What happens:** TAP TO PLAY → title Home with a rocket on the pad and a big **LAUNCH KH-1** (good) → a mission brief with a grey desktop scrollbar → **Build it** → a designer that's already **Ready ✓** → **LAUNCH** → a 12 s countdown (`LiftoffHandoff.OpenAt = -12`) with jargon chips (Internal, Sequencer, Deluge) and a card saying **"89 m/s short for First Hop"** → liftoff → about 25 s of climb with telemetry and a "Nudge pitch -1°" prompt → the report card covers the rocket at engine cutoff and leads with a red **Fix it**.

- **Taps to first liftoff:** 4 (TAP TO PLAY, LAUNCH KH-1, Build it, LAUNCH). It's 5 if the player uses Skip count.
- **Time to first liftoff:** about 4 s of boot after TAP TO PLAY (localhost), plus reading two screens, plus the 12 s count. That's roughly 20 to 25 s for a player who doesn't read and 40 to 60 s for one who does.
- **Why it doesn't land:**
  1. The player makes no decision before the first flight.
  2. The countdown says the rocket is short just after the designer said it was ready.
  3. The payoff (reaching 10 km, then "and back") is never shown, because the report cuts in at cutoff.
  4. The first thing on the result screen is a red Fix it, even though the flight passed.
- **What should change (engine-agnostic):**
  - Give KH-1 one meaningful tap before launch, such as a rocket that's one notch short with a big "More fuel" control that fills the speed bar green with a sound.
  - Make the first countdown 5 s, or start it at T-5.
  - Hold the camera through a short apogee moment ("10 km!" banner, stars pop).
  - Open the report with MISSION COMPLETE and a single big **Next mission**.

## Smaller notes (not ranked)

- The Home status dots (○ ●) on the LAUNCH and Missions cards have no meaning a kid can read. Label them or remove them. Seen in build: `01`.
- In landscape, the stat-bar values and the progress-rail labels (Liftoff, Max-Q, MECO, Fairing) are about 10 px, and the coach line sits at the bottom edge at about 10 px. Seen in build: `16`.
- The failure report says the same fact four times ("8.00 g, past the 8.0 g limit" ×4). Peak equal to the limit reads as "past". It also advises "set a g limit", but the Starter panel has no g-limit control. Seen in build: `13`.
- "Heating hard" shows on a 10 km hop at about 300 m/s, which is an alarm with nothing to do. Seen in build: `16`.
- The briefing payload model pops in from a placeholder texture about 4 s after the screen opens. Seen in build: `02` (compare with `p01` in the raw run).
